Overview & Strategic Context

About Scotties

Scotties has been a community staple for snowboard and skateboard enthusiasts in Whitby/Durham since 1993, built on passion, technical tuning expertise, and grassroots culture.

The Core Challenge

Despite strong local loyalty, Scotties relied on an outdated single-page website with no e‑commerce capability. Out-of-town customers could not browse or buy gear, and the store struggled to compete digitally against big-box sporting chains without losing its authentic street-culture identity.

Brand Message & Positioning

"Scotties: Ride Local, Dream Big." Making extreme sports accessible through trusted expertise, affordable options, and a community-first experience that large chain stores cannot replicate.

Core Value Proposition & Emotional Benefits

To differentiate Scotties from generic mass-market chains, our UX/UI system was designed around three central emotional pillars:

Confidence (Expert Guidance): Replicating the in-store staff’s trusted advice digitally so customers feel assured they are investing in the right setup.

Belonging (Community Hub): Welcoming newcomers and veterans alike into a passionate, tight-knit culture through integrated local event feeds and rider stories.

Excitement (Subculture Discovery): Celebrating the thrill of discovering unique local partnerships and niche gear through high-energy visual layouts.

Visual Strategy

Tactile Street-Culture Moodboard

For the moodboard, we curated visuals that capture the raw energy, passion, and movement inherent to skate and snow culture. By contrasting vibrant action photography with outdoor trail elements, the visual board sets an approachable yet high-spirited tone for the entire e‑commerce experience.

Typography & Color Palette

Brand Identity: Logo Concept

The redesigned logo anchors Scotties’ identity by synthesizing kinetic motion, outdoor context, and youth vitality into a unified mark. The fluid, elongated initial "S" doubles as a kinetic trail carved by skateboards on pavement and snowboards down slopes, set beneath a sharp alpine silhouette that directly contextualizes the store’s outdoor performance roots. Crisp, angular lines convey extreme speed, adrenaline, and youthful energy, while the solid, uppercase Nimbus Sans Bold wordmark grounds the dynamic geometry with geometric stability and cross-platform clarity. Rendered in signature vibrant orange (#FC702F) and deep olive green (#515839), the mark strikes an intentional balance between action-sports intensity and rugged alpine heritage.

Lo-fi Homepage

Initially, our priority was driving online conversions, so we framed the homepage after traditional athletic retail layouts to emphasize products and seasonal sales.

The primary navigation directly mirrored our core sport divisions—Skateboarding, Snowboarding, and Skiing—allowing visitors to jump straight into product browsing.

Lo-fi Product Listing Page

Added secondary filters below for gender, price range, active sales/offers, and deck colorway to support targeted browsing.

Centered clean card modules featuring high-contrast product imagery alongside essential gear specifications and pricing.

Hi-fi Homepage

Following client discussions, we evolved the homepage strategy beyond a standard transactional layout. Because Scotties’ true value lies in local stickiness—providing gear, expert repairs, and organizing rider events—the high-fidelity design was restructured to heavily emphasize its grassroots, community-first identity.

Opens with high-energy action sports photography anchored by the core tagline, "Ride Local, Dream Big," immediately immersing visitors in an authentic subculture vibe.

Positioned strategically just below the fold, this section highlights top-tier flagship items across all three core categories (Skate, Snow, Ski) with clear pricing to capture high-intent buyers and drive immediate sales.

Transitions gracefully from commerce to storytelling by introducing the shop’s roots and specialized tuning services. This guides users toward the About page to build brand trust through expert staff profiles and 24-hour maintenance offerings.

The final layout block acts as a cultural anchor, featuring dynamic photo galleries of past meetups and teasers for upcoming skate/snow jams. This section actively funnels enthusiasts into the Community page, fostering long-term brand loyalty and offline engagement.

Key Learnings & Strategic Reflection

Community as a Moat

Unlike big-box sporting goods chains, a local independent shop’s competitive advantage lies in building lasting, grassroots relationships rather than merely pushing products. Our design strategy needed to reflect this distinct positioning by shifting away from a purely transactional e‑commerce layout. By integrating dedicated modules for specialized repair services, staff tuning expertise, and upcoming local events, we transformed the website into an active community hub that drives local engagement and customer retention.

Visualizing Subculture Energy

Catering to skateboarders and snowboarders means speaking directly to an active, young-at-heart, and passion-driven demographic. Every visual touchpoint—from high-energy color tokens (#FC702F Orange) and kinetic typography (Nimbus Sans & CCThatsAllFolks) to organic lifestyle photography—had to authentically channel the raw adrenaline, youthfulness, and dynamism of extreme board sports without compromising digital usability.

"By harmonizing e‑commerce functionality with grassroots board culture, this project demonstrated how digital design can protect a local shop’s authentic community soul while empowering it to grow as a competitive modern brand."
